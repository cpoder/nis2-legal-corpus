"Repeals ZInfV" is true and incomplete. The old act ceases to have effect on
2025-06-19, but its security requirements, supervision and penalties keep
applying for one year to two groups (Art. 62(2) and (3)). A repeal with
transitional survival is not a plain repeal, and must not be rendered as one.

## Scope: essential and important entities, national additions, exits

Read in full: Art. 3, Art. 5 (74 definitions), Arts. 6 and 7, Annexes 1 to 3.
Three features decide real cases: the general size test is **cumulative** (50
employees **and** EUR 10 M), the essential-entity size test is **disjunctive**
(250 employees **or** EUR 50 M **or** EUR 43 M), and several categories are in
at any size. The general test is also narrower than the directive's own
yardstick, in both directions (last rows of the table).

## Sector routing in the product: both halves of Art. 59 (2026-10-29)

Art. 59 (quoted in the incident section above) gives SIGOV-CERT two remits:
"subjektov javne uprave na državni in lokalni ravni" and "ponudnikov storitev
zaupanja, ki jih izvajajo subjekti državne uprave". The configuration carried

SIGOV-CERT automatically, and a digital-infrastructure entity keeps SI-CERT and
is shown SIGOV-CERT as a condition to check. The channel is unchanged: e-mail to
cert@gov.si or cert@cert.si, as URSIV's guidance of 2026-06-03 says. The legal
basis shown for the national recipient is Art. 30(1) and Art. 59.
