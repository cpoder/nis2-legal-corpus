**The date of entry into force, computed.** The act was promulgated in ДВ
бр. 17 of Friday 13 February 2026 and sets no date (read in full). Art. 5(5)
of the Constitution puts it in force three days after promulgation. Counting
the three days from the day after publication (14, 15 and 16 February) and
ending the term at 24:00 on the 16th, as Art. 41(3) and (4) of the
Statutory Instruments Act (ЗНА) provide, the act took effect on
**17 February 2026**. What is verified: the act is silent, the
Constitution's three-day rule is in force, and the Constitutional Court
counts the three days the same way: in its Decision No 7 of 11 August 1994
a silent act promulgated on Friday 22 July 1994 is in force on Tuesday
26 July 1994, and the 2026 act was promulgated on Friday 13 February 2026
(read on 2026-11-05 at 05:14 UTC, after the rest of this record). What is
located but not verified is only the wording of the counting rule, ЗНА
Art. 41(3) and (4), read in a non-official consolidation (APIS, hosted on
the Дряново District Court's site). The
gazette's online archive starts in 1990 and does not hold the 1973 act; the
2007 and 2016 amending acts, read at the gazette, do not touch Art. 41(2) to
(4); the 1995 and 2003 amendments were not read. In no reading is the date
13 February. The June 2026 act (ДВ бр. 55/2026) likewise sets no date (its
text searched for "в сила": one hit, § 93, a six-month deadline counted from
"влизането му в сила"), so the ministry's new name runs from three days after
16 June 2026, 20 June 2026 by the same count.

### What the scouting note of 2026-06-07 said, and what the texts say

The note (kept in git history) recorded "Entry into force = 2026-02-13
(promulgation day)", status "resolved", on the reasoning that the act is
silent and that "under the ZNA default (the 3-day rule, Art. 5, was repealed
in 2007 with no replacement) a law silent on EIF takes effect on its
promulgation date". It called the "2026-02-17" of secondary sources a
misapplication of a repealed rule.

- **The three-day rule is in the Constitution, Art. 5(5), and is in force.**
  The National Assembly's text of the Constitution lists amendments of 2003,
  2005, 2006, 2007, 2015 and 2023 and Constitutional Court decisions
  published in 2006 and 2024, and Art. 5(5) still reads as quoted above.
- **What was repealed in 2007 is Art. 5 of the Statutory Instruments Act**
  (§ 7 of ДВ бр. 46/2007, quoted above). Its content was not read here; it is
  not the constitutional rule, and nothing in the 2007 act makes silent laws
  take effect on promulgation.
- **The secondary sources' 17 February 2026 is the date the counting rule
  gives.** The note's two facts that stand: the act is silent (confirmed), and
  it was adopted on 5 February 2026 by the 51st National Assembly (confirmed).

The error lived on two other surfaces. The BG row of the transposition
tracker (`packages/regulatory-data/src/nis2-transposition-status.ts`) had
`enforcementDate: "2026-02-13"` and a note saying the act "takes effect on the
promulgation date (ZNA default; the old 3-day rule was repealed in 2007);
secondary '17 Feb' claims misapply the repealed rule". The row was corrected on
2026-11-05, after this record: it says 2026-02-17, its note states the
constitutional rule and the Court's count, and
`packages/regulatory-data/test/bg-entry-into-force.test.ts` holds each step
against the saved texts (its `nationalLawDate` of 2026-02-13 is the
publication date and was right as such). The BG row of
`docs/legal-sources/README.md` said "resolved → EIF 2026-02-13 (act silent;
"17 Feb" debunked)" and was corrected with this record.

## Departures from the directive, read from the texts above

<!-- meta: theme=scoping,registration,incident,governance,sanctions,framework; type=reference; date=2026-11-05; surfaces=docs; guard=none -->

- **The 24 and 72-hour clocks run from "установяването"** (establishment) of
  the significant incident, not from "узнаването" (awareness), and the act
  omits "без ненужно забавяне" (Art. 23(5), т. 1 and 2).
- **The recipient is the sectoral CSIRT only** (Art. 23(1) and (5)); the
  directive allows the CSIRT or the competent authority.
- The report for a still-ongoing incident is a "междинен доклад", not a
  "доклад за напредъка" (Art. 23(5), т. 5).
- **Management bodies train every two years**, and must offer and organise
  the training for employees (Art. 21(2) and (3)); the directive says
  "regularly" and "encourage". Their members face a personal fine of EUR 500
  to 5,000 for breach of Art. 21 (Art. 29(4)).
- **Minimum fines**: at least EUR 25,000 (essential) and EUR 12,500
  (important) (Art. 29(2) and (3)); fines halved for breaches before 1 June
  2026 (§ 51).
- **Every administrative body is essential, whatever its size** (Art. 4,
  т. 1; Art. 4а(1), т. 4), an administrative body being any body of the
  executive power (§ 3, т. 1 of the supplementary provisions); the
  directive's Art. 3(1)(г) names as essential the public administration
  entities of its Art. 2(2)(е)(i), central government.
- **The judiciary is in scope** (Art. 4, т. 9), supervised by the Supreme
  Judicial Council (§ 55); so are public-function holders and public-service
  organisations for their e-administrative services (Art. 4, т. 7 and 8).
- NIS1 operators of essential services are essential if designated "към
  датата на влизане в сила на настоящия закон" (Art. 4а(1), т. 7); the
  directive's option is for entities identified before 16 January 2023.
- **No self-registration duty with a deadline**: the competent authorities
  designate the entities under a Council of Ministers methodology (Art. 16(3),
  т. 8; §§ 47, 48); the register is not public (Art. 6(5)).
- Two measures beyond the directive's ten (Art. 22(2), т. 11 and 12); a
  national power to restrict risky technologies with a three-year phase-out
  (Art. 27).
- Heads of strategic sites also notify the State Agency for National
  Security's centre at once (Art. 15(6)).
- The precedence clause names national special laws, not Union acts
  (Art. 6а); DORA appears as cooperation duties (Arts 16(13), 17(12), 27м).
- Public administration is not an Annex I sector; it is reached through
  Art. 4, т. 1.
- The size test is the directive's: the SME Act's Art. 3(1), now in the
  Recommendation's euro figures, with the public-control rule disapplied as
  the directive does (not a departure; recorded because it is reached
  through a national act).
