### Notes

- The law is an amendment to the pre-existing NIS1 transposition law (89(I)/2020), not a standalone new law.
- Article 57(1): entry into force on the day of publication in the Official Gazette = **2025-04-25**.
- Article 57(2): Art. 7 provisions (new registration mechanism for MSPs/public administration entities) enter into force on a date set by Council of Ministers by notification — this deferred commencement means the EC's 7 May 2025 reasoned opinion for "failure to notify full transposition" likely targeted this deferred article 7 (and possibly the notification delay to the Commission). The core NIS2 obligations are in force from 2025-04-25.
- The EC Cyprus page (last updated 2025-07-07) still shows the reasoned opinion; the nis-2-directive.com tracker (updated 2026-03-12) states legislative transposition is complete.
- cylaw.org is the official Cyprus legal database operated by the Republic of Cyprus, mirroring the Official Gazette (Επίσημη Εφημερίδα).
