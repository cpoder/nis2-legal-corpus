### What the scouting note of 2026-06-07 said, and what the texts say

The note was written from the CyLaw copy and quoted the right articles, but
four of its claims do not survive the full reading:

- **"Art. 7 provisions (new registration mechanism for MSPs/public
  administration entities)" is false.** Article 7 of the amending law
  rewrites the Authority's power to accept grants, and since the correction of
  25 June 2025 the deferred provision is article 8, which deletes the base
  law's staffing board. Neither concerns registration. The registration
  provisions (art. 27(3) and (4), 27Β, 37Α, 38Α) are in force since
  25 April 2025 (registration section). The note's own quote of 57(2) was
  also cut short, and it quoted the text as first published, a year after the
  correction of 25 June 2025.
- **The short title is spelled «Ασφάλειας»** in the gazette, not
  «Ασφαλείας».
- **"cylaw.org is the official Cyprus legal database operated by the Republic
  of Cyprus" is false.** CyLaw describes itself as the service of the Cyprus
  Legal Information Institute (ΚΙΝΟΠ/CyLII), paid for by the Cyprus Bar
  Association; the official publication is the Government Printing Office's
  gazette (sources section).
- **The note's reading of the reasoned opinion of 7 May 2025** (that it
  "likely targeted this deferred article 7") was a guess and is withdrawn; the
  register only records the dates, and the case is closed.

The first error also lived on two other surfaces. The CY row of the
transposition tracker (`packages/regulatory-data/src/nis2-transposition-status.ts`)
said in its note "the Art. 7 registration mechanism is deferred to a
Council-of-Ministers date", and was corrected on 2026-10-30. The CY row of
`docs/legal-sources/README.md` said "Art.7 registration deferred to CoM date" and is corrected with this
record.

## Scope: who is obliged, the size test and the two classes

<!-- meta: theme=scoping; type=reference; date=2026-10-30; surfaces=data,prompt; guard=packages/regulatory-data/test/cy-incident-reporting.test.ts,packages/regulatory-data/test/nis2-mappings-loader.test.ts,packages/regulatory-data/test/national-size-tests.test.ts -->

The Cypriot law copies the directive's scope rules almost word for word (art.
2Α, inserted by Ν. 60(Ι)/2025 art. 4) and its two classes (art. 27,
replaced by art. 19): «βασικές οντότητες» (essential) and «σημαντικές
οντότητες» (important), the very terms of the directive's Greek text. Two
national features decide real cases: **the classes are conferred by a
designation**, a list approved by a classified decision of the Council of
Ministers and a Decision of the Authority served on each entity (art.

## Incident reporting: what, to whom, by when, and through what

<!-- meta: theme=incident; type=reference; date=2026-10-30; surfaces=data,ui,prompt,mcp,doc-gen; guard=packages/regulatory-data/test/cy-incident-reporting.test.ts,packages/regulatory-data/test/calendar-months.test.ts,apps/core-api/test/incident-acts-cy.test.ts,apps/core-api/test/incidents-cy-routes.test.ts,apps/core-api/test/incident-reminders-cy.test.ts -->

The law keeps the acts of the directive's Art. 23(4) (early warning,
notification, interim report on request, final report, progress report) but
makes **the first one due within six hours, not 24** (art. 35Β(4)(α)), and replaces the directive's single
progress report with **a progress report every fifteen days** while an
incident is still running at the final-report date, the final report then
being due **fifteen days after restoration** (35Β(4)(ε)). The anchor is the
directive's own Greek wording, «από τη στιγμή που αντιλήφθηκαν»: awareness.
Everything goes **to the Authority**, which forwards it to the national CSIRT
(CSIRT-CY, part of the Authority). Κ.Δ.Π. 281/2026, in force since 3 July 2026,
sets the procedure, the significance thresholds, the form and the channel.

The acts and their Greek names: «ενημέρωση» (the law's (4)(α), called
«έγκαιρη προειδοποίηση» in its (5)(α) and in the form, «ενημέρωση (early
warning)» in the decision) within 6 hours; «κοινοποίηση περιστατικού» within
72 hours (24 hours for trust service providers); «ενδιάμεση έκθεση» on request;
«τελική έκθεση» one month after the notification; «έκθεση προόδου» every 15
days for an ongoing incident.

## Departures from the directive, read from the texts above

<!-- meta: theme=scoping,registration,incident,governance,sanctions,framework; type=reference; date=2026-10-30; surfaces=docs; guard=none -->

Each line is a reading of quotes already recorded in this file and in eu.md;
it adds no source.

- **Incidents, the first act within 6 hours, not 24** (35Β(4)(α); Κ.Δ.Π.
  281/2026 art. 4(6)(β)), for every essential and important entity, trust
  service providers included (their 24 hours replace only the 72-hour
  notification). The anchor is the directive's Greek wording, awareness
  («αντιλήφθηκαν»); the decision words the clock from detection only for an
  incident notifiable through media coverage alone (Annex I proviso).
- **Ongoing incidents: a progress report every 15 days**, then the final report
  15 days after restoration (35Β(4)(ε)), instead of one progress report and a
  final report one month after handling; an interim report is also due
  whenever the data change materially (Κ.Δ.Π. 281/2026 art. 4(6)(στ), (ζ)).
- **National significance thresholds** for every type other than the digital
  ones (Κ.Δ.Π. 281/2026 Annex I: 5,000 user-hours, 20,000 for
  e-communications; a whole municipality or community; five persons seriously
  injured; any death; one security objective; media coverage, elections,
  politically exposed persons); the digital types follow Implementing
  Regulation (EU) 2024/2690 (Annex III).
- **Duties the directive does not have**: keep incident information 24 months
  after the final report (art. 9 of the decision); name 24/7 reporting contacts
  within 20 working days (art. 10); get the Authority's approval for not
  informing recipients of a significant cyber threat (35Β(2) proviso); newly
  established entities ask the Authority about security by design (27Α);
  entities in scope that are not on the list inform the Authority (27Β).
- **Classes by designation**: a criticality assessment on criteria approved by
  the Council of Ministers (27(1)(β)), a list approved by a classified Council
  of Ministers decision (27(7)), and a Decision of the Authority served on each
  entity (27(8)).
- **Wider than the directive**: former critical information infrastructure
  operators are essential, not only former operators of essential services
  (27(1)(α)(vii)); local public administration is in (2Α(5)), where the
  directive leaves it optional; the wider public sector replaces the regional
  level (2Α(2)(στ)(ii)).
- **Narrower or silent**: the Cyprus Intelligence Service is entirely outside
  (2(5)); no counterpart was found to the directive's Art. 2(10) (entities
  exempted from DORA under its Art. 2(4)).
- **Management**: its members must offer similar training to their employees
  regularly (35Α(3)), where the directive has Member States encourage the
  entities; officers share liability for paying administrative fines (base law
  54(5)); the temporary ban (36Α(5)(β)) is the directive's, essential entities
  only, never public administration.
- **Sanctions**: the directive's fine levels (43Α(4), (5)), with the Greek
  text's slip in Art. 34(4) corrected; next to them, the 2020 law's own fines
  (EUR 200,000 and EUR 10,000 a day, EUR 300,400, EUR 5,000) and **criminal
  offences** for not notifying (two years or EUR 10,000) and for not taking
  measures (three years or EUR 15,000) (44(1), (3)), besides the 2020 law's
  other offences (44(5) to (7), 20(3)).
- **Information**: the Authority may ask for logs covering at least six months
  (17(κη)).
- **Registration**: no self-registration deadline and no mechanism found;
  changes within two weeks for the list (27(4)), three months for the digital
  registry (37Α(3)), as in the directive.
- **Measures**: no national NIS2 catalogue; the NIS1 framework Κ.Δ.Π. 389/2020
  addresses the former operators of essential services and of critical
  information infrastructure, and the maturity audits of Κ.Δ.Π. 245/2024
  (registered auditors, paid by the entity, report in Greek) those operators,
  e-communications providers, digital service providers and any entity
  designated under the law.
- **Financing**: essential and important entities finance the Authority through
  Regulations of the Council of Ministers (base law art. 11(1)(α) as amended).
