## Scope: who is obliged, the size test and the two classes

<!-- meta: theme=scoping; type=reference; date=2026-10-29; surfaces=data,prompt; guard=packages/regulatory-data/test/nis2-mappings-loader.test.ts,packages/regulatory-data/test/national-size-tests.test.ts -->

The act calls the obliged party a "verksamhetsutövare" (operator) and splits
operators two ways: "väsentlig" or "viktig" (the directive's essential and
important), and "offentlig" (state agency, region, municipality, kommunalförbund)
or "enskild" (everyone else). Six routes bring an operator into scope (1 kap.
3 to 8 §§); one paragraph classifies them (1 kap. 9 §).

## Defect: the scope check classed every Swedish region and municipality as an important entity (found and fixed 2026-11-05)

<!-- meta: theme=scoping; type=scope-error; date=2026-11-05; surfaces=data,ui,mcp,prompt,docs; guard=packages/regulatory-data/test/national-size-tests.test.ts,apps/ai-service/test/auto-scoping-national-rules.test.ts,packages/regulatory-data/test/public-scoping-conformance.test.ts -->

The act puts every region, municipality and kommunalförbund under it,
whatever its size (1 kap. 3 § 2), and makes a municipality or a region larger
than a medium enterprise an essential entity (1 kap. 9 § 2 a); whoever is not
essential is important (1 kap. 9 § second paragraph). The rows are in "Scope:
who is obliged, the size test and the two classes" above.

The scoping engine applied the directive's reading to Sweden. A regional or a
local administration came out as an important entity whatever its size, and a
municipality was told that it was in scope "only where the member state has
extended the directive to local administration" and to "check the national
transposition". A municipality of several thousand staff was therefore classed
as important: on the public scope checker, in the product's own scoping and
through the MCP tool. The English country page already stated the act's rule,
so the engine contradicted the page.

Since 2026-11-05 the engine carries the act's rule
(`NATIONAL_PUBLIC_ADMINISTRATION` in
`packages/regulatory-data/src/scoping.ts`):

| Answer | Verdict | Rests on |
|---|---|---|
| Regional or local level, larger than a medium enterprise | essential | 1 kap. 3 § 2 and 9 § 2 a |
| Regional or local level, not larger than a medium enterprise | important, in scope whatever the size | 1 kap. 3 § 2 and 9 § second paragraph |
| Central level, or no level stated | essential | 1 kap. 9 § 1, for a state authority under 1 kap. 3 § 1 or 8 § |

What it does with the open item. The act defines a medium enterprise by the
Recommendation (1 kap. 2 § 13) and the bill reads "larger than a medium
enterprise" as at least 250 persons, or a turnover above EUR 50 million and a
balance sheet total above EUR 43 million (rows above). No text read says
which figures of a municipality or a region count (open items below). The
engine applies the bill's line to the figures entered and says so in the
verdict: "Neither the act nor the bill says how a region's or a municipality's
figures are counted, so the figures entered are taken as given." The item
stays open.

What the questionnaire cannot tell apart, and the verdict says:

- A **kommunalförbund** answers "local" like a municipality. 9 § 2 a names
  municipalities and regions only, and the authority's guidance classes a
  kommunalförbund as important: the local verdict says so in both classes.
- A **county administrative board** (länsstyrelse) is a state authority under
  1 kap. 8 § and the ordinance's 4 §, not a region: the regional verdict says
  so, and that it is essential.
- A **state authority** is under the act only by 1 kap. 3 § 1, 8 § or one of
  the general routes of 4 to 7 §§: the central verdict names the first two
  and says one of them is assumed.

The auto-scoping prompt states the same rule, and the published scoping
specification has a section and six cases for it (`se-01` to `se-06`).

## Security measures: the duty, MCFFS 2026:11, the PTS sectors, the supply chain and audits

Sweden has a national measures catalogue in one föreskrift, MCFFS 2026:11, in
force since 2026-10-01, with binding provisions and non-binding general advice.
It does not apply to operators active **solely** in the five PTS sectors, which

telecoms, space and postal, PTS föreskrifter not yet issued; for them only the
management-training chapter of MCFFS 2026:11 applies. The act adds no
classification grade: the same catalogue binds essential and important
operators.

## Incident reporting: what, to whom, by when, and through what

<!-- meta: theme=incident; type=reference; date=2026-10-30; surfaces=data,prompt; guard=packages/regulatory-data/test/se-incident-reporting.test.ts,packages/regulatory-data/test/nis2-mappings-loader.test.ts -->

Since 2026-10-30 this section feeds `INCIDENT_REPORTING_SE`
(packages/regulatory-data/src/country-configs/incident-reporting.ts): the
awareness anchor, the four acts, the CSIRT at FRA and the Cyberportal, whose
landing page was read live again that day ("Cyberportalens
rapporteringstjänst ska användas av verksamhetsutövare för att rapportera
betydande incidenter enligt cybersäkerhetslagen (2025:1506)"). Until then
Sweden ran on DEFAULT, which pointed at the ENISA directory. The same day,
when Swedish became a product locale, every label of the configuration gained
a Swedish version in the act's own words (upplysning, incidentanmälan,
delrapport, lägesrapport, slutrapport, "har fått kännedom om", CSIRT-enheten
at FRA, Cyberportalen), held by
`packages/regulatory-data/test/config-official-languages.test.ts` (SE: sv)
and by the Swedish case of `se-incident-reporting.test.ts`.

The act follows the directive's four acts in its own Swedish names:
**upplysning** (24 h), **incidentanmälan** (72 h, 24 h for trust services),
**delrapport** (on request), **slutrapport** (one month after the
incidentanmälan) or **lägesrapport** then slutrapport. The act anchors the
24 and 72 hours on "fått kännedom om incidenten"; the föreskrift and the
authority word the same moment as the incident being identified as
significant. The recipient is FRA's NCSC (CERT-SE), through the Cyberportal;
NCSC forwards to the supervisors.

## Departures from the directive, read from the texts above

<!-- meta: theme=scoping,registration,incident,governance,sanctions,framework; type=reference; date=2026-10-29; surfaces=docs; guard=none -->

Each line is a reading of quotes already recorded in this file and in eu.md;
it adds no source.

- **Public administration goes down to the local level.** Every
  municipality, region and kommunalförbund is in at any size (1 kap. 3 § 2),
  where the directive requires central government, and regional entities on a
  risk-based assessment, and allows the local level (Art. 2(2)(f), 2(5)(a), as
  read in eu.md); every state agency in scope is essential (9 § 1); the
  preparedness agencies are added by name (8 §, ordinance 4 §).
- **Private degree-awarding education providers** of at least medium size are
  in (1 kap. 4 § 1), essential when large (9 § 2 c).
- **Inverted ladder: IXPs** are treated as public e-communications networks,
  in at any size and essential from medium size (bill p. 48, MCF0027 p. 45),
  where the directive size-tests them as an Annex I type.
- **Registrars become important operators** (7 § 4 with 9 § second paragraph),
  with the full duties of 2 kap., where the directive keeps them outside both
  classes (Art. 2(4), 3(3)). Reading; PTS asks them for the basic measures.
- **Size-independent cases set by föreskrift**: emergency airports, air
  traffic control, quarantine ports and protected places, drinking water to
  20,000 persons or an acute hospital (essential); critical chemical additives
  (important) (MCFFS 2026:1 4 kap.); and an exemption for group companies that
  are large only through the group (3 kap. 1 §, recital 16).
- **CER critical entities are not yet in scope** (directive Art. 2(3), 3(1)(f));
  the CER bill would add them from 2027-01-01 (Prop. 2025/26:303).
- **Registration**: no fixed deadline ("så snart det kan ske"); changes in 14
  days for everyone, matching Art. 3(4) and shorter than the three months
  Art. 27(3) allows for the digital types' information (comparison section).
- **Management**: the act carries only the training duty; approval and
  oversight are in MCFFS 2026:11 3 kap. 4 § (the bill saw no need for a
  provision in the act);
  no personal-liability clause beyond the ban, which is limited to private
  essential operators, after an unheeded order, for intent or gross
  negligence, for one to three years (4 kap. 6 to 8 §§).
- **Incidents**: the act's anchor is the directive's ("fått kännedom"); the
  föreskrift and the authority word it as identification of the incident as
  significant. National thresholds for most sectors (MCFFS 2026:8) and for
  telecoms (PTSFS 2026:5); the duty to inform users of incidents affecting
  external services is made firmer by the föreskrift (5 kap. 1 §). State
  agencies keep a 6-hour first report under the preparedness ordinance.
- **Fines**: for private operators, maxima at the level Art. 34(4) and (5)
  require (the directive sets no cap of its own: eu.md, section of
  2026-11-05); a SEK 10 million cap for public operators (Art. 34(7) leaves
  this to the Member State), a SEK 5,000 floor; the act's turnover limb names
  no undertaking (directive Art. 34(4)).
- **Supervision**: sector supervisors plus six county boards by geography; a
  supervision fee for e-communications providers (3 kap. 11 §, PTSFS 2026:4).
- **Measures**: a national catalogue (MCFFS 2026:11) for every sector outside
  the PTS sectors, binding essential and important operators alike; MFA,
  joining CERT-SE's ANTS and DNSSEC are named duties; supplier contracts made
  before 2026-10-01 to be supplemented "om möjligt".
