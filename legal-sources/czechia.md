## Scope: the regulated service, the size test and the two regimes

<!-- meta: theme=scoping; type=reference; date=2026-10-29; surfaces=data,docs; guard=packages/regulatory-data/test/national-size-tests.test.ts -->

The Czech act does not list "essential and important entities". It regulates
**services**: a service becomes a regulated service when NÚKIB decides so
(§ 3), after the provider notifies it (§ 6). Whether the conditions are met is
decided by the annex of decree 408/2025, row by row. Each provider of a
regulated service is in one of two regimes, higher or lower obligations (§ 8).

## Defect: the scope check classed a Czech region as an important entity (found and fixed 2026-11-05)

<!-- meta: theme=scoping; type=scope-error; date=2026-11-05; surfaces=data,ui,mcp,prompt,docs; guard=packages/regulatory-data/test/national-size-tests.test.ts,apps/ai-service/test/auto-scoping-national-rules.test.ts,packages/regulatory-data/test/public-scoping-conformance.test.ts -->

Row 1.1 of the annex to decree 408/2025 lists the public bodies that provide
a regulated service, in two lists and with no size condition (rows in "Public
administration (annex row 1.1)" above). A region ("s) kraj") and the capital
city of Prague ("t) hlavní město Praha") are in list I, the regime of higher
obligations, which the decree's explanatory memorandum equates with the
directive's essential entities.

The scoping engine applied the directive's reading to Czechia. A regional
administration came out as an important entity. A municipality came out as an
important entity with the instruction to "check the national transposition",
although the decree answers: only a municipality with extended powers and
Prague's districts 1 to 22 are listed, in the regime of lower obligations.
Both country pages, the Czech and the English one, already stated the two
lists, so the engine contradicted the pages.

Since 2026-11-05 the engine carries row 1.1
(`NATIONAL_PUBLIC_ADMINISTRATION` in
`packages/regulatory-data/src/scoping.ts`):

| Answer | Verdict | Rests on |
|---|---|---|
| Regional level | essential, whatever the figures | row 1.1, list I, letter s; the memorandum's "režimu vyšších povinností (essential entities)" |
| Local level | important, a listed municipality being assumed | row 1.1, list II, letters e and f; § 18(4) of the act for the pairing of the lower regime with important entities |
| Central level, or no level stated | essential, a listed body being assumed | row 1.1, list I |

What the questionnaire cannot tell apart, and the verdict says:

- A **municipality the row does not list** does not provide the regulated
  service of public administration. The questionnaire asks the level of
  government, not the kind of municipality, so the local verdict assumes a
  listed one and names which are listed. It can overstate, never understate.
  The engine's verdict for a level of administration has no "not regulated"
  outcome; an answer for the kind of municipality would need one.
- A **state administrative office with regional, district or other
  territorial competence** is in list II (letter a), the regime of lower
  obligations. It answers "regional" like a region: the regional verdict
  says it is in the lower regime instead.
- The **capital city of Prague** is in list I (letter t): both the regional
  and the local verdict say so.

The other service rows of the decree stay outside the engine, as the section
"Scope: the regulated service, the size test and the two regimes" says. The
auto-scoping prompt states the same rule, and the published scoping
specification has a section and four cases for it (`cz-01` to `cz-04`).

## Incident reporting: what, to whom, by when, and through what

<!-- meta: theme=incident; type=reference; date=2026-10-29; surfaces=data,ui,prompt; guard=packages/regulatory-data/test/cz-incident-reporting.test.ts,apps/core-api/test/incident-deadlines-cz.test.ts,packages/regulatory-data/test/nis2-mappings-loader.test.ts -->

Three features decide real cases. The **higher regime reports every incident**
that meets three conditions, significant or not, and NÚKIB says within 24 hours
whether it is significant; the lower regime reports only incidents it assesses
as significant. **Both report only incidents whose intentional cause cannot be
ruled out** (the § 18 digital services excepted). And the recipient depends on
the regime: NÚKIB for the higher, the National CERT for the lower.

## Departures from the directive, read from the texts above

<!-- meta: theme=scoping,registration,incident,governance,sanctions; type=reference; date=2026-10-29; surfaces=docs; guard=none -->

Each line is a reading of quotes already recorded in this file and in eu.md; it
adds no source.

- **Scope is by registered service, not by entity type.** Duties attach to a
  provider of a regulated service from the delivery of NÚKIB's registration
  decision (§§ 3, 6, 13(4), 15(4)); the measures and reporting duties start up
  to a year after it.
- **Two regimes instead of two classes**, one per organisation; the higher
  regime matches essential entities (§ 18(4), memorandum). The annex keeps the
  directive's Annex II types lower-only (patterns B and B'), with two upgrades:
  series production of passenger cars is higher at any size (7.4), and research
  in the listed sensitive fields is higher (19.1).
- **Narrower than the directive**: authoritative-only DNS for 10,000
  second-level domains or fewer is out (16.4; the directive covers DNS
  providers regardless of size); incidents whose intentional cause can be
  excluded are not reportable (§ 15(1), (2); the directive's significant
  incident has no such condition), except for the § 18 digital services.
- **Broader than the directive**: the higher regime reports every incident of
  the § 15(1) kind, not only significant ones (§ 15(1), § 16(2)); national
  additions in the annex (16.5 .cz registrars, 16.7 gov.cz, 16.11 eID, 16.18
  National CERT, 17.4 and 17.5 payment and e-money institutions, 3.7 filling
  stations, 2.10 electricity data centre, 18.2 emergency medical services,
  21.x defence industry, municipalities with extended powers, Prague 1 to 22,
  universities, professional chambers, the Academy of Sciences, the Czech
  National Bank, the Constitutional Court, the offices of both chambers, health
  insurers).
- **Deadlines**: the final report is due 30 days after the notification, not
  one month (§ 16(3)(c) against Art. 23(4)(d)); for an ongoing incident the act
  asks for a status report without undue delay after the period, where the
  directive asks for a progress report within it ("v uvedené lhůtě",
  Art. 23(4)(e)).
- **Size test**: the Recommendation applies by reference with the directive's
  own deviation (Art. 3(4) off, § 7(a)) and three national ones: state units,
  municipalities, regions and the ČNB are not enterprises (§ 7(b)); partner or
  linked enterprises with fully separate technical assets are not counted
  (§ 7(c)); research bodies that are not enterprises are sized by analogy
  (§ 7(d)).
- **Users**: informing users of a significant incident is the provider's own
  judgement unless NÚKIB orders it (§ 19(1)), where the directive says "where
  appropriate".
- **Management**: training of top management is initial and regular ("vstupních a pravidelných školení") with no
  fixed interval in either decree; the temporary ban exists only in the higher
  regime and only after a breached remedial order (§ 58).
- **Fines in CZK**: CZK 250 M or 2 % and CZK 175 M or 1.4 % (§ 59(4)), and the
  top band also for anyone who fails to notify a service (§ 60(7)(a)).
- **A national supply-chain mechanism** for strategically significant services
  (§§ 25 to 33), not operative while its government regulation is missing.
