## Scope and classification: the directive's size test, by reference

<!-- meta: theme=scoping; type=reference; date=2026-10-28; surfaces=data,ui,marketing; guard=none -->

The size test is the directive's, applied by reference to Recommendation
2003/361/CE. Nothing national departs from it, unlike Slovenia's cumulative test:
the deterministic scoping engine therefore needs no Luxembourg rule.

## Risk-management measures, the annual deliverables, and why there is no national baseline

<!-- meta: theme=framework,governance,supply-chain; type=reference; date=2026-10-28; surfaces=data,ui,marketing,prompt; guard=packages/regulatory-data/test/country-configs-transposition-references.test.ts -->

**Readiness verdict (2026-10-28): SUBSTRATE.** The act is in force, but no
national technical baseline is adopted. Art. 12(2) lists ten measures in general
terms and leaves the risk-analysis framework and the notification of measures to
an ILR regulation or circular; the ILR consulted on two draft
regulations in July and August 2026, and on 2026-10-28 the ILR's NIS pages listed
neither as adopted. So a Luxembourg entity runs the NIS 2 substrate, as the Netherlands and
Austria do. Revisit when an ILR regulation under Art. 12(1) or 12(3) is published.

## Incident notification: to the competent authority, through SERIMA

<!-- meta: theme=incident; type=reference; date=2026-10-28; surfaces=data,ui,marketing,prompt,mcp; guard=packages/regulatory-data/test/country-configs-incident-reporting.test.ts,apps/web/test/authority-report-hints.test.ts -->

The Luxembourg particularity: the entity notifies the **competent authority**
(the ILR, or the CSSF within its remit), never the CSIRT directly. The authority
forwards to the CSIRT and to the single point of contact. Filing with CIRCL is
the mistake to prevent. The clocks run from awareness ("après avoir eu
connaissance").

## The ILR's plain-language size test joins its criteria with « ou »

<!-- meta: theme=scoping; type=reference; date=2026-10-28; surfaces=docs,marketing; guard=none -->

The act applies the directive's size test by reference to Recommendation
2003/361/CE (Art. 1(1), above). The ILR's self-registration page explains it in
its own words, and those words do not say what the Recommendation says.

| Fact | Where | Verbatim (official) | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| The ILR's explanation of "medium-sized" | ILR, "Auto-enregistrement" (FR), https://www.ilr.lu/secteurs-activites/niss/auto-enregistrement/ | "Une entité est considérée comme moyenne si elle a un effectif d’au moins 50 et pas plus de 249 personnes, ou si elle a un chiffre d’affaires annuel entre 10 millions € et 50 millions €, ou si le total du bilan annuel est entre 10 millions € et 43 millions €." | verified (read 2026-10-28) |
| The same, in the ILR's English version | ILR, "Self-registration" (EN), https://www.ilr.lu/en/sectors/niss/self-registration/ | "An entity is considered medium-sized if it has at least 50 and no more than 249 employees, or if it has an annual turnover of between €10 million and €50 million, or if its annual balance sheet total is between €10 million and €43 million." | verified (read 2026-10-28) |

Read literally, the sentence makes medium-sized an enterprise of 30 persons with
a turnover of EUR 20 million and a balance sheet total of EUR 8 million. The
Recommendation makes it small: "a small enterprise is defined as an enterprise
which employs fewer than 50 persons and whose annual turnover and/or annual
balance sheet total does not exceed EUR 10 million" (Annex Art. 2(2), verified in
`eu.md` §F, where "a turnover above EUR 10 million on its own makes an enterprise
medium-sized" is recorded as refuted). In the other direction, an enterprise of
300 persons with a turnover of EUR 40 million is "moyenne" for the sentence and
large for the Recommendation ("fewer than 250 persons", Annex Art. 2(1)), which
for an Annex I type is the difference between important and essential (Art.
11(1) 1°).

The act refers to the Recommendation, not to the ILR's page. The product
therefore keeps the Recommendation (the deterministic scoping engine, unchanged
for Luxembourg) and the `/fr-lu` pages quote neither set of figures: they send
the reader to the Recommendation. Not a product defect; recorded so that nobody
"aligns" the scoping engine or a page on the ILR's sentence.
